In a moonlit valley, crystal trees rise like translucent spires, their glass bark shimmering with emerald veins that pulse rhythmically as wind whispers through them. A soft pink aurora hums in the distance, blending with the cyan glow of reflected rain, while deep black silhouettes of distant hills frame the scene. Meteor showers cascade overhead, each fragment a silver streak cutting through the midnight sky, casting fractured light onto the forest floor. The composition leans toward a dramatic diagonal, guiding the eye from the luminous meteor tips down through the winding canopy toward a shimmering pool of crystal water. The style feels hyper‑realistic yet dreamlike, with precise textures and a subtle, almost musical atmosphere.
