A vast expanse of pale salt flats stretches under a bruised twilight sky, the horizon a line of beige dunes that blend into a deep turquoise horizon where the remnants of ancient wooden hulls rise from the white sand, their weathered timbers speckled in copper and coral stains. A lone ship, half buried, glows faintly as if illuminated from below by a ghostly glow. The composition balances the horizontal sweep of the desert with the vertical lines of the ship’s masts, framed by distant gray cliffs that cast long, soft shadows across the scene. The overall style is hyperrealistic, with a subtle painterly texture that evokes a dreamlike, post‑apocalyptic atmosphere.
